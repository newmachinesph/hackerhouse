# HackerHouse Accelerator — Design Brief

## Goal

Build an editorial, motion-led site for a Manila live-in business residency. The site should feel selective, operational, and intense without reading like a nightclub, dating product, or generic startup landing page.

## Audience

Founders and independent operators who already run a full-time business, make at least PHP 150,000 per month, and want to pursue one measurable 10× target over a year.

## Brand system

- Name: HackerHouse Accelerator
- Location: Manila, Philippines
- Palette: carbon black, bone white, signal red
- Typography: compressed display headlines, restrained sans-serif body copy, monospaced labels
- Voice: direct, specific, selective, and free of borrowed competitor wording
- Photography: dark, lived-in work environments; builders separated by task; no party or stock-team energy

## Experience

- Scroll-led home narrative with parallax, staged chapter changes, and a persistent progress rail
- Readable copy at every motion state; text never enters before its background surface has settled
- Distinct route identities for Thesis, House, Rules, and Cost
- Existing photography reused where it advances the story; no hero image repeated near the footer
- New imagery added only when the existing set cannot carry a distinct editorial beat
- Video requires explicit approval before generation or integration

## Required pages

- Home
- Thesis
- House
- Rules
- Cost

## Responsive requirements

- Phone: 375 × 812
- Tablet: 768 × 1024
- Desktop: 1280 × 800
- No horizontal overflow, clipped display type, hidden first paint, or decorative labels crossing key content
